Theme 1 — Swiss Modern. White canvas, black type, one red signal color, visible column grid. Most neutral and "Google-clean" of the five.
Theme 2 — Blue Professional. Warm cream paper, one cobalt accent, soft tinted cards. Consulting / B2B SaaS feel; closest to your Phase 1 brief.
Theme 3 — Signal. Deep navy with an antique-gold accent and an editorial serif headline. Most "boardroom" of the five; very legible on a projector.
Theme 4 — Booth Editorial. Ivory paper, Booth maroon spine, classic Caslon headline. Nods to Chicago Booth without looking like a school template.
Theme 5 — Graphite. Product-style dark mode with a muted teal accent and mono labels. Feels like a modern product launch; pairs well with a live demo.
